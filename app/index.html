    Read this before Chapter 1 ends

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy any guide flags as possibly missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia invites you to meditate on the balcony at Jason’s house. Do that meditation before leaving Chapter 1, and keep a separate Chapter 1 save as insurance — PowerPyx is still checking whether skipping it blocks the trophy. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

    Planning notes

    • One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free roam continues after the final mission.
    • Keep cheats off — using a cheat disables trophies and saving.
    • Keep a manual save at each chapter boundary. Missions cannot be replayed.
    • The 100% checklist is closer to 60% true completion: PowerPyx notes you need 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animals studied and 50 of 66 weapons, not all of them.
    • Time budget from the guides: story 60–80 hours, 100% checklist about 100 hours, Strangers & Freaks and clean-up about 10 hours — roughly 200 hours to the platinum.
